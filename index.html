Method & assumptions
Detailed deflection to AS 3600 Cl 8.5.3 / 9.3.3. Section cracked-transformed properties give Icr; Mcr = (0.6√f′c − σcs)·Z. Effective stiffness by Branson: Ief = Icr + (Ig−Icr)(Mcr/Ms)³ ≤ Ief,max (Ig if Ast/bd ≥ 0.005, else 0.6 Ig). Immediate δ = β·w·Lef⁴/(EcIef); long-term adds kcs·δsustained, kcs = 2 − 1.2(Asc/Ast) ≥ 0.8. Continuous members use span-region Ms; AS 3600 permits averaging Ief. Two-way uses a short-span strip with a load-share factor — a simplified idealisation; verify important slabs by rigorous/FE analysis.
